## Overview
Endless discovery awaits you with Sentosa's golden beaches, tranquil getaways and thrilling theme parks.

## Atmosphere & Themed Experiences

### Sentosa, An Island of Endless Discovery
Sun, sand and surf await you on Sentosa, with adventure at every corner. 
   
 Spend your mornings cycling along scenic coastal paths, then dive into thrills at Universal Studios Singapore, the Skyline Luge, or immersive marine encounters at the Singapore Oceanarium. 
   
 As the sun sets, beach clubs, ocean-view dining and luxury resorts set the tone for relaxed evenings by the water.

### A Paradise of Sun, Sand and Surf
Sentosa's beaches each have their own unique charm. People watch while sunbathing at Siloso, enjoy family fun parks at Palawan or visit Tanjong Beach for a tranquil getaway.

### Fun for the Family
Thrills aplenty await the entire family at Sentosa, from iconic restaurants like Coastes to the unforgettable rides at Universal Studios Singapore.

### Adventures into Nature
Whether you're cruising around on a Segway or ziplining above the treeline, Sentosa's natural beauty is a sight to behold.

## What to Do

### Harry Potter: Visions of Magic
Explore the mysterious corners of the wizarding world through Harry Potter: Visions of Magic, an interactive art experience inspired by places in the novels and film series.

### Resorts World™ Sentosa
From underwater wonders to live events and fine dining, Resorts World™ Sentosa is full of excitement and enchantment.

### Sensoryscape
Embark on a sensory journey from Sentosa's lush ridges to its vibrant reefs, in this day-to-night experience.

### The Palawan @Sentosa
This beach wonderland promises fun for all! Explore Asia’s first indoor gamified electric go-kart circuit, Singapore’s only floating aqua park, an 18-hole mini-golf course, beach clubs, iconic food trucks and more.

### Singapore Cable Car
Travel to and from Sentosa in style, and discover spectacular views aboard the Singapore Cable Car ride.

### AltitudeX Singapore
Feel the rush of the wind through your head and the adrenaline through your veins at the world's largest wind tunnel for indoor skydiving.

### SkyPark Sentosa
Test your mettle as you hurtle through the air with SkyPark Sentosa's bungee jump and other high-flying attractions.

### Mega Adventure Park
Ride tandem with your little daredevils on the zipline, trampolines and obstacle courses at this adventure park.

### SentoSights – Sentosa Naturalist Night Adventure
Discover the sights and sounds of the rainforest after dark, with this nocturnal jaunt on Sentosa's wild side.

### SentoSights – Introduction to Birdwatching
Pick up the basics of birdwatching and admire the island's feathered inhabitants with this fascinating foray into nature.

## Where to Shop

### LEGO® Certified Store
Selling all things LEGO, Bricks World carries a wide range of LEGO products, from figurines to car models!

### Hershey’s Chocolate World Singapore
Bring home a sweet gift at Hershey’s Chocolate World, from Hershey’s, to Reese’s and Twizzlers.

### Jasons The Gourmet Grocer
Choose from a wide selection of groceries and epicurean brands at Jasons, a gourmet shopping paradise.

## Where to Eat

### FOC by the Beach
Kick back, indulge in delicious Mediterranean eats and sip on Sangrias at this beachside restaurant.

### Good Old Days Food Court
Indulge in laksa, hokkien mee or other delicious local eats at this halal-certified food court.

### Quentin's Eurasian Restaurant
From fiery curry  debal  to hearty cod fritters and roasted pork belly, this restaurant is famous for authentic Eurasian fare.

### Ocean Restaurant
Discover what it's like to dine beneath the deep blue sea, as you dine alongside majestic sea creatures like sharks and manta rays.

## Where to Stay

### Amara Sanctuary Sentosa
Nestled within lush confines, Amara Sanctuary Sentosa has won numerous awards for its conservation efforts. Housed in a colonial style building, it offers nine room types.

### Capella Singapore
Luxury awaits at the Capella Singapore. Find tranquility in a tropical oasis, with gourmet restaurants to choose from.

## Overview
An inspiring blend of hip meets heritage, Chinatown is home to trendy eateries, buzzing watering holes and old-school charm.

## Atmosphere & Themed Experiences

### History of Chinatown
Get clued in on both hip haunts and traditional treasures on the busy streets of Chinatown. Visitors can head to the Thian Hock Keng or Buddha Tooth Relic temple for an afternoon of quiet reflection, and visit herbal medicine stores and antique shops selling Chinese art, and jewelry for plenty of souvenir options. 
   
 Chinatown also appeals to curious food lovers, with Maxwell Food Centre and Chinatown Complex offering dishes from different cuisines, and restaurants and cocktail bars known for their locally inspired menus.

### The Melting Pot
Dig into all that Chinatown has to offer, from its rich history to its tantalising mix of diverse cultures.

### Old Meets New
Soak in the buzz of Chinatown's busy streets or slow down to admire its exquisite temples – no matter your passion, Chinatown is bound to deliver.

### Savour the Flavour
Mouthwatering Michelin meals and delectable hawker fare are just some of the flavours you can sample in Chinatown.

## What to Do

### Chinatown Heritage Centre
Step back in time at Chinatown Heritage Centre, rebuilt from memories of early residents who lived in these shophouses. Immerse in their stories of struggles and dreams.

### Smith Street
Experience Smith Street, where Chinatown's heritage meets modern energy through traditional shops, eateries, creative spaces, and cultural activities that bring traditions to life.

### Chinatown Murders (牛车水 Murders)
This murder mystery tour through Chinatown is filled with intriguing clues and untold stories.

### Trails of Tan Ah Huat
Hop on a bicycle and let Tan Ah Huat – a fictitious coolie – bring the streets of Chinatown to life.

### Growing Up - A Mural Walk in Chinatown
Discover Chinatown's past with an art walk featuring murals by Yip Yew Chong. Don’t forget to snap away!

### Buddha Tooth Relic Temple & Museum
Home to a sacred relic, this majestic temple is a trove of Buddhist wisdom.

### Masjid Jamae
One of our city's oldest mosques, Masjid Jamae is famous for its eastern and western infused architecture.

### Sri Mariamman Temple
Step into Singapore’s oldest Hindu temple to admire the ornate carvings and soak in the vibrant atmosphere.

### Thian Hock Keng Temple
Singapore’s oldest Chinese temple is steeped in heritage and is a must-visit for history aficionados.

### Singapore Art Museum
Singapore's pioneering art museum is home to the work of bright young artists and regional names alike.

## Where to Shop

### Chinatown Street Market
More than just a shopping stretch, this iconic street is home to a wide range of offerings, from lacquered fans to charming souvenirs.

### Yue Hwa Building
Go treasure hunting in Yue Hwa building – this charming department store is chock full of anything you'd desire, from traditional herbs and calligraphy brushes to tasty snacks.

### Bee Cheng Hiang
With humble street hawker roots that date back to the 1930s, Bee Cheng Hiang serves up delicious slices of freshly barbecued meat.

### Kele
Starting out as a humble bakery nearly four decades ago, Kele is now one of Singapore's most popular snack brands. Sample their pineapple tarts and savour the tradition!

### Pek Sin Choon
Talk about an old-school pick me up. Pek Sin Choon has been selling its range of fragrant, traditional tea for almost a century.

### On Cheong Jewellery 
From jade bangles and gold necklaces to beautifully-crafted rings, this traditional jeweller boasts a range of masterful designs.

### Binary Style
Shop from a homegrown lifestyle brand, renowned for its vibrant prints inspired by local architecture and nature.

### Chop Wah On
Soothe your aches at Chop Wah On, one of Singapore's oldest medicated oil makers. Since 1916, this family business crafts healing remedies and traditional balms.

### Eu Yan Sang
Experience traditional wellness at Eu Yan Sang, a trusted name in Chinese medicine for over 145 years. Discover time-tested herbal remedies and wellness products.

## Where to Eat

### Chinatown Complex
Eat your way through the delectable dishes at Chinatown Complex, Singapore's largest food centre. Keep your eye out for the Michelin Bib Gourmand-awarded hawker stalls!

### Maxwell Food Centre
A beloved stomping ground of local foodies, Maxwell Food Centre is home to hawker delights like chicken rice.

### Zén
Bjorn Frantzén's three Michelin-starred restaurant is famed for its masterful take on Scandinavian and Japanese fusion cuisine.

### Native
Sample the flavours of Asia at one of Asia's 50 Best Bars – the unique cocktails here are concocted with ingredients and spirits from Singapore and across the region.

### Tong Heng Traditional Cantonese Pastries 
Some of the toothsome pastries from Tong Heng include candied melon pastries, savoury buns and their signature egg tarts.

### Kok Sen Restaurant
This Michelin Bib Gourmand-awarded restaurant is run by the third generation and serves up a tantalising range of Cantonese cuisine.

### Restaurant Espoir
Restaurant Espoir blends European culinary artistry with bold flavours of Malayan herbs and spices, offering a modern Halal dining experience with delightful surprises in every bite.

## Where to Stay

### Clan Hotel
Modern luxury meets old-school heritage in the heart of Chinatown, with Clan Hotel's immaculate service and well-appointed rooms.

### PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering, Singapore
Besides exquisite amenities and world-class hospitality, PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering, Singapore is famed for its award-winning designs for sustainability.

## Overview
With its soaring skyscrapers and gorgeous skyline, the Marina Bay we see today is made up of 38 hectares of reclaimed land.

## Atmosphere & Themed Experiences

### Modernity at Marina Bay
Discover the glittering, modern side of Singapore as you explore trendy Marina Bay and its eateries, hotels, and shopping malls.

### A Shimmering Skyline
Architectural icons and majestic landmarks like the Singapore Flyer abound in the Marina Bay district, some of which line the Marina Bay Waterfront Promenade.

### In the Lap of Luxury
Whether you're looking to shop, indulge in top restaurants or enjoy a theatrical performance, Marina Bay is bound to deliver.

### Experience Tomorrow Today
From the ArtScience Museum to the soaring SuperTrees, Marina Bay's attractions offer a glimpse of the future.

## What to Do

### Merlion Park
Your visit to Singapore isn't complete unless you've snapped a photo with this iconic landmark! Located at Merlion Park, this majestic icon symbolises our nation's rich history as both fishing village and Lion City.

### Esplanade - Theatres on The Bay
From rock music to ballet and theatre, this beloved art centre is home to an array of world-class performances and productions.

### Gardens By The Bay
Nature’s beauty is unveiled with Gardens by the Bay’s surreal sights, paradisal spaces and lush greenery.

### Singapore Flyer
See the world from a brand new perspective at the Singapore Flyer. Asia's largest observation wheel stands 165 metres above ground, with cabins that offer stunning views of the city.

### Marina Barrage
Both a water source and a popular haunt for local families, this charming spot boasts wide open spaces that are perfect for picnicking and kite-flying.

### Marina Bay Sands
Live the high life at this iconic resort, with luxury accommodation, upscale shopping and an infinity pool that boasts unparalleled views of the city.

### ArtScience Museum
Shaped like a lotus flower, this museum is where art meets tech. Step into dreamy digital worlds or explore mind-bending exhibitions. Kids love it, and so do adults.

## Where to Shop

### The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands
With over 170 luxury brands – from Tiffany & Co to Acne Studio's first-in-Asia store – The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands is a great place to indulge in a bout of retail therapy.

### Millenia Walk
From luxury brands to tech gadgets, Millenia Walk boasts a smorgasbord of diverse shopping opportunities.

### Suntec City Mall
One of Singapore's largest malls, this sprawling space is home to something for the entire family, from watches and beauty products to toys for the little ones.

### Esplanade Mall
Discover the Esplanade Mall's diverse retail scene, offering a variety of stores from music to boutiques to suit your taste.

## Where to Eat

### Republic Bar
Imbibe on a liquid journey with a narrative of cocktails devoted to the 1960s at the award-winning Republic Bar.

### LeVeL 33
Enjoy a mug of artisanal beer and an unforgettable view at LeVeL 33, the world's highest microbrewery.

### Waku Ghin
Helmed by Tetsuya Wakuda, this two-Michelin-starred restaurant serves up  omakase  (chef-selected) style meals made with immaculate craftsmanship and exquisite produce.

### Labyrinth
The brainchild of chef-owner LG Han, this Michelin-starred restaurant elevates local flavours with whimsical modern twists.

### Kok Kee Wonton Noodles (MBS outlet)
Dig into oodles of noodles and enjoy a beloved hawker staple at this popular restaurant.

### Satay by the Bay
Feast your senses at Satay by the Bay, where the aroma of satay and hawker favourites meet the breathtaking Marina Bay views and lush Gardens by the Bay scenery

### Marguerite
A feast for the senses, this Michelin-starred restaurant is located amid the lush beauty of Gardens by the Bay, and boasts equally stunning interiors.

### MO Bar
With a breathtaking view of the skyline and delicious cocktails, a visit to MO Bar promises to be a heady experience.

## Where to Stay

### Marina Bay Sands 
Indulge in high-end amenities and unparalleled service at Marina Bay Sands, where you will find an infinity pool and an observation deck overlooking the city's skyline.

### The Fullerton Bay Hotel
The Fullerton Bay Hotel Singapore promises you a luxurious stay, with a panoramic sweep of the Marina Bay waterfront.

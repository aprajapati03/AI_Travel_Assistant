## Overview
Wander from the historic streets of Kampong Gelam to the vibrant Bras Basah and Bugis district, and uncover a world of street art, cultural icons, surprising food finds and hidden gems — set amid temples, museums and contemporary design.

## Atmosphere & Themed Experiences

### Trend and Traditions of Kampong Gelam
From the vibrant street art along Haji Lane to the majestic charm of Sultan Mosque, you will find an interesting blend of Chinese, Malay, Indian and European cultures all in Kampong Gelam. 
   
 The adventure continues at its surrounding districts Bras Basah and Bugis, which is known for its cultural landmarks, colonial-era buildings, shopping districts and street markets.

### History and Heritage Abound
One of Singapore's oldest urban quarters, this district is filled with fascinating stories from the past. Bugis, for example, was a bustling place of trade and entertainment.

### Where New-Age Meets Old School
Kampong Gelam is a neighbourhood of bold new boutiques and esteemed artisans, trendy cafes and traditional eateries.

### Museums and Street Vibes
After a day at the creative heart of Singapore, where art schools and museums are, the party starts as the sun sets at Kampong Gelam - with street art, live music, and supper spots.

## What to Do

### Sultan Mosque
An iconic architectural landmark, the impressive Sultan Mosque is a focal point for Singapore’s Muslim community.

### Hajjah Fatimah Mosque
Also known as Masjid Hajjah Fatimah, the Hajjah Fatimah Mosque will captivate you with its blend of cross-cultural architectural influences.

### #instawalk: Bugis, Waterloo, Kampong Gelam
Take a jaunt around Bugis, Waterloo Street and Kampong Gelam, explore hidden gems and snap a trove of Instagram-worthy shots for your social media feeds.

### Haji Lane
Singapore's original indie neighbourhood is a great place to wander, with its hole-in-the-wall bars, boutique fashion stores and indie businesses. Keep a look out for wall murals and graffiti by both local and internationally-renowned artists.

### A Painted Sip
A beginner-friendly immersive painting workshop where participants can paint and customise their own reusable tumblers and choose from a range of Singapore-inspired designs.

## Where to Shop

### Kebaya by Ratianah
Masterfully-tailored from exquisite fabric, the kebaya and the baju kurung from Ratianah Tahir's boutique are bound to brighten up your wardrobe.

### Jamal Kazura Aromatics
Fall in love with the world of perfume at Jamal Kazura Aromatics, a traditional perfumery that's been around since 1933. Discover a perfume that speaks to your personality or create your own signature scent.

### Hygge
Classical designs and elegant minimalism exude from the trinkets, accessories and decor on display at Hygge. Take home a dose of cosiness, and find the ideal gift for your loved ones at home.

### Crafune
Get inspired to work with your hands, and pick up basics of leather-working at Crafune, where you can learn how to make your very own accessories, from wallets to handbags.

### Bugis Street
A bustling street market that sells everything from quirky knick knacks to fashionable garments. Scour the best deals through shops packed with clothes, shoes and accessories.

### Goodluck Bunch
If you're a fan of street culture, you're going to love the effortlessly cool vibes at Goodluck Bunch, with its selection of hoodies, sneakers and apparel for both men and women.

### Toko Aljunied
Intricate  batik  fabrics and gorgeous, hand-sewn garments are on display at this traditional store, with a history that dates all the way back to 1940.

## Where to Eat

### Hjh Maimunah
Dig into food that tastes just like your makcik (aunty) made it at Hjh Maimunah. This Michelin Bib Gourmand eatery is famous for its nasi padang – rice with an assortment of delicious side dishes, from beef rendang to sambal prawns.

### Rumah Makan Minang
Run by the third generation, the rich and diverse flavours of Indonesian cuisine are on full display at Rumah Makan Minang. Enjoy delicious traditional fare like  tahu telur  and  mee soto.

### The Coconut Club
Dig into a plate of  nasi lemak  – a traditional rice dish made with a variety of side ingredients. The version whipped up by The Coconut Club, a Michelin Bib Gourmand recipient, elevates this hawker staple with a modern spin on its ingredients.

### Bar Stories
This cozy watering hole offers bespoke cocktails to thirsty tipplers, with a variety of drinks inspired by your response to the bartenders' questions.

### Ji Xiang Ang Ku Kueh
Ji Xiang is a local brand that specializes in Ang Ku Kueh, a traditional Chinese pastry made with soft and chewy glutinous rice flour. Located right opposite Bugis Junction, this store is one of its 10 outlets in Singapore.

### Bugis HaveFun LiveShow 
Immerse yourself in the electrifying atmosphere at HaveFun Live Show at Bugis+. Enjoy an unforgettable night with bar delights and close-up live performances!

## Where to Stay

### The Sultan 
Admire the columns and full-length shuttered French windows of the conserved buildings that houses The Sultan, and then step inside to immerse yourself in a world of comfort.

### Andaz Singapore
Treat yourself to thoughtfully designed guestrooms with stunning views of the city, at the first Andaz-branded property in Southeast Asia.

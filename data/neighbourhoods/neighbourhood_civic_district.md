## Overview
Skyscrapers and colonial buildings sit side by side in the Civic District, home to some of the island's finest museums and art galleries.

## Atmosphere & Themed Experiences

### The Civic District – Heritage and Modernity
Formerly the heart of colonial administration in Singapore, the Civic District's fascinating past lives on in the present day. 
   
 Grand buildings, carefully conserved landmarks, and museums line the district, inviting visitors to slow down and appreciate Singapore’s historic grandeur set against modern urban everyday life.

### Realms of Arts and Culture
With its many galleries and museums, the Civic District is the perfect destination for those who appreciate arts and culture.

### Senses and Sensibilities
Treat yourself to posh new pleasures in the Civic District, from shopping sprees to sumptuous cuisine.

### Tours, Tipples and Treasures
Sip on a Singapore Sling, discover the heart of the city on a tour and admire architectural gems from Singapore's storied past.

## What to Do

### Peranakan Museum
Discover the vibrant world of Peranakan culture with this museum's thought-provoking exhibits and rare artefacts on display.

### National Gallery
Situated in Singapore's Former City Hall and Supreme Court, this art gallery houses the world's largest collection of Southeast Asian art.

### National Museum of Singapore
Housed in a beautiful colonial-era building, our oldest museum boasts a range of exhibitions and galleries that tell the story of our nation through modern technology.

### Asian Civilisations Museum
From the salvaged cargo of a Tang dynasty shipwreck to religious statues and ceramics, the Asian Civilisations Museum is a trove of the region's diverse cultures.

### Victoria Concert Hall
The magnificent Victoria Concert Hall dates back to the 19th century and plays host to a range of theatre productions, concerts and performances.

### Singapore Street Food and Night Tour 
It's not just our city lights that sparkle after dark – discover our city's UNESCO-awarded hawker culture on this jaunt through Singapore's vibrant night life.

### Historical Singapore Bicycle Tour 
Discover the sights and sounds of modern Singapore and its storied history on a bicycle. Take a trip to a local wet market, discover landmarks in the heart of the city and try cycling through the legendary Formula 1 track!

### Fort Canning Park
Once known as  Bukit Larangan  (Forbidden Hill), Fort Canning Park’s history is closely intertwined with that of the Lion City. Explore the Heritage Gallery and expansive lawns as you take on a self-guided walking tour.

## Where to Shop

### SUPERMAMA The Museum Store
From plates adorned with local motifs to adorable coasters that celebrate local cuisine, this homegrown brand is a great option for shopping local.

### Raffles City Shopping Centre
With over 200 specialty shops from fashion labels to watches and jewellery, this shopping centre is an ideal spot for luxury shopping.

### Bugis Street
Snack on street food and hunt for great bargains on fashion and accessories at Bugis Street's 600 shops.

### Funan
Hunt for tech gadgets and more at Funan. Besides shopping, this futuristic mall also boasts a climbing wall, rooftop urban farm and an indoor cycling lane.

### Food Folks @ Lau Pa Sat 
You'll find a ton of scrumptious local eats at Lau Pa Sat, including stores like Food Folks, which serves up comfort dishes, gifts and yummy local snacks.

### Raffles Arcade
Located in the iconic Raffles Singapore hotel, this arcade is home to a range of stores selling everything from cameras to jewellery and chic apparel.

## Where to Eat

### National Kitchen
Located within the National Gallery, the brainchild of local celebrity chef Violet Oon serves up delicious Peranakan cuisine and a breathtaking view of the city skyline.

### Lau Pa Sat Food Centre
Also known as Telok Ayer Market, Lau Pa Sat is both a striking national monument and bustling hawker centre. Be sure to drop by in the evenings to sample the satay along Boon Tat Street!

### HighHouse
Offering an unparalleled 360-degree views of the Singapore skyline, HighHouse is a transcendent day-to-night nightlife and culinary destination where music, art, flavours, and conversations intersect.

### NOVA
Singapore’s highest rooftop bar with unobstructed panoramic views blends magical sunsets and sublime nights into multisensory experiences - evoking an atmosphere of a festival in the sky.

### Chjimes
Formerly a Catholic convent, this timeless landmark is now home to various restaurants and cafes like Whitegrass, New Ubin Seafood and The Glasshouse.

### JAAN By Kirk Westaway 
Experience Michelin excellence at two-star restaurant JAAN by Kirk Westaway, high atop Swissotel with stunning views.

### Long Bar 
Explore the timeless allure of Long Bar, renowned for being the birthplace of the legendary Singapore Sling cocktail.

## Where to Stay

### The Capitol Kempinski Hotel 
Bringing out the neoclassical-style Capitol Building and Venetian Renaissance-style Stamford House, The Capitol Kempinski Hotel offers you a luxury retreat in the heart of our Civic District.

### Lyf Funan Singapore
Located in Funan Mall, lyf Funan gives you access to an array of business and entertainment attractions, as well as a host of heritage sites and opulent art galleries.

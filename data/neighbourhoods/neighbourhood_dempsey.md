## Overview
Surrounded by greenery, Dempsey is a serene, heritage-rich area known for its colonial-style architecture, unique dining, and eclectic lifestyle experiences.

## Atmosphere & Themed Experiences

### Singapore's Heritage Hideaway
Encompassing the Dempsey, Minden, and Loewen clusters, Dempsey is a blend of history, heritage, and modern allure. Once home to military barracks, the area has since transformed into a charming area filled with eateries, art spaces, and specialty shops.

### Echoes of the Past
Discover Dempsey's storied past, from its 19th-century barracks to a historic chapel and former army quarters reimagined as vibrant lifestyle spaces.

### A Feast for the Senses
Nestled in nature, Dempsey offers everything from Michelin-starred dining to farm-to-table experiences.

### Playful Discoveries
Beyond its calm setting lies a trove of activities. Go on a whimsical journey at the Museum of Ice Cream or taste Singapore's award-winning gin at Tanglin Gin Distillery.

## What to Do

### Kids Space
More than a playground, Kids Space is a dedicated space where children can explore and learn through thoughtfully designed kids' programmes.

### Museum of Ice Cream
The only outpost outside the USA, this whimsical space invites you into pastel-hued wonderlands, endless scoops, and exclusive treats.

### Nowhere Baths
A contemporary gender-neutral bathhouse, Nowhere Baths offers hot baths, cold plunges, a sauna, and a steam room. Each experience is designed to soothe muscles, quiet the mind, and promote recovery.

### REDSEA Gallery
One of Singapore's leading contemporary art galleries, REDSEA presents an ever-changing collection of works from Asia and beyond.

### Tanglin Gin Distillery
Taste Singapore's gin legacy where it all began, with a chic bar that serves their signature pours.

## Where to Shop

### BEÁ Style Curators
A boutique of discovery, BEÁ Style Curators offers hand-picked clothing and accessories from independent Brazilian labels.

### B.P. de Silva Jewellers
Crafting heirlooms since 1872, B.P. de Silva Jewellers specialises in bespoke creations and ready-to-wear fine jewellery rooted in timeless heritage.

### Dover Street Market Singapore
A fashion haven of luxury houses, cult streetwear, and exclusive collaborations, Dover Street Market Singapore is where style meets avant-garde.

### Kids21
From designer apparel to accessories, Kids21 curates luxury fashion for little ones with an eye for quality and style.

### Mr. Bucket Chocolaterie
Dig into decadent chocolate, discover unique flavours, and learn how this local brand creates delicious treats from cacao beans with a factory tour.

### The Dempsey Project
A unique combination of a deli, grocery store, and eatery, The Dempsey Project champions gourmet produce and wholesome meals sourced sustainably from around the world.

## Where to Eat

### Air CCCC
AIR CCCC is a farm-to-table restaurant, cooking club, and research campus rolled into one - a place where hearty food meets inspiring ideas.

### Burnt Ends
Known for its wood-fired craft, Burnt Ends pairs Michelin-star precision with the smoky soul of Australian barbecue.

### Canchita Peruvian Cuisine
Authentic Peruvian and Latin American fare come alive at Canchita, where vibrant ceviches, shared plates, and lush greenery create a lively communal dining atmosphere.

### Candlenut
A one Michelin star restaurant, Candlenut serves up traditional Peranakan fare with a creative twist and an Indonesian touch.

### Chopsuey Café
Where East meets West, Chopsuey Café serves Anglo-Chinese classics in a charming colonial black-and-white setting.

### Claudine
Set in a beautifully restored 1930s chapel, Claudine brings French brasserie dining to life with warm hospitality and refined flavours.

### Jiu Zhuang
Step into the 1920s Shanghai lifestyle with Jiu Zhuang, an oriental-inspired bar where premium drinks are paired with Chinese dim sum (small bite-sized dishes).

### Jumbo Seafood Dempsey
Enjoy a flavourful seafood meal in the tranquil, lush Dempsey enclave. Don't forget to dig into Singapore's iconic chilli crab while you are there.

### Samy's Curry
Founded in the 1960s and located at Dempsey for over 50 years, Samy's Curry offers authentic South Indian cuisine served on banana leaves.

### Torno Subito
Offering a cinematic slice of the 1960s Adriatic Riviera, Torno Subito serves Italian classics that showcase Chef Massimo Bottura's wit and creativity.

## Where to Stay

### Goodwood Park Hotel
A heritage icon since 1900, Goodwood Park Hotel charms its guests with colonial-era elegance and modern luxury—right in the heart of Orchard Road.

### The Standard, Singapore
The Standard turns heads with its striking décor and playful personality. Just minutes from Dempsey, its vibrant pool deck, buzzing Kaya bar, and chic social spaces invite you to linger and mingle at every corner.

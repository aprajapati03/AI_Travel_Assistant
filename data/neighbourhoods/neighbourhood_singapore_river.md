# Singapore River - Singapore Neighbourhood Guide

**Source Title**: Singapore River Neighbourhood Guide - Visit Singapore
**URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/singapore-river/
**Category**: Featured Destination Neighbourhood

---

## Overview
From delectable food to buzzing nightlife, the Singapore River's trio of quays are bound to deliver a wave of exciting experiences.

## Atmosphere & Themed Experiences

### Adventures Along The Singapore River
With a history that intertwines with our city and a modern day lifestyle scene that buzzes with excitement, this iconic district is well worth a visit.

### A Family Getaway
Robertson Quay is a great family hangout, with its eclectic mix of alfresco cafes and restaurants, and the scenic boat ride on the Singapore River Cruise.

### A Place to Party
Clarke Quay is the beating heart of Singapore's vibrant nightlife scene, with a range of popular clubs where you can party past midnight.

### From Restaurants to Bars
With international restaurants, buzzing bars and charming cafes, you'll never be short of a good meal at Boat Quay.

## Practical Visitor Information

- **Nearest Train Station**: Clarke Quay (NE5)
- **Prayer Facilities**: Masjid Omar Kampong Melaka

## What to Do

### Kulnari Mystery Golf
Hole in ones meets mysterious historical intrigue at Kulnari Mystery Golf, a charming blend of mini-golf course and escape room shenanigans.

### Slingshot
You don't even have to look to find this ride – Just listen for the screams of exhilaration. Strap in for a rush, this ride reaches speeds of 160 kilometres per hour!

### Zouk
Home to world-class DJs and nonstop party vibes, this legendary venue is a stalwart of Singapore's nightlife scene.

### Asian Civilisations Museum
From the salvaged cargo of a Tang dynasty shipwreck to religious statues and ceramics, the Asian Civilisations Museum is a trove of the region's diverse cultures.

### Evolve MMA
Get your gloves up – whether you're looking to pick up Muay Thai strikes or Brazilian Jiu Jitsu holds, Evolve MMA is a great place to get started.

### Prestique Studio
Bounce to your heart's content at Prestique Studio! Both fun and great for fitness, the unique workouts here get your heart rate up through the use of mini-trampolines.

### Singapore River Cruise 
Cruise down the Singapore River, learn about our city's rich history and take in the gorgeous sights of our skyline. You can even enjoy dinner on the water while enjoying the experience.

### Singapore Street Food and Night Tour
It's not just our city lights that sparkle after dark – discover our city's UNESCO-awarded hawker culture on this three and-a-half hour long jaunt through Singapore's vibrant night life.

## Where to Shop

### Clarke Quay Central
You'll find fashion boutiques and stores selling a range of gifts, accessories and gadgets at this conveniently-located mall.

### Clarke Quay Street Stalls
With its bustling vibes and ever-changing lineup of trinkets, this is a great place to pick up a souvenir.

### Riverside Point
Shop for gifts and soak in the bustle along the riverside at this charming locale.

## Where to Eat

### Offtrack
Art, food and music come together at Offtrack, an effortlessly cool bar that serves up Pan Asian cuisine, cocktails and live DJ sets.

### Shabestan
From juicy beef kebabs to hearty Persian stews, the dishes at this eatery along the Singapore River are bound to get your mouth watering.

### Jiak Kim House
Discover Singapore’s culinary heritage in a beautifully restored 1920s Singapore River warehouse. Savour bold Modern Asian dishes that celebrate the best of East and West.

### Quay House
Nestled in the heart of Boat Quay, Quay House honours its spice trading past with Asian tapas inspired by the Maritime Silk Route.

## Where to Stay

### 21 Carpenter
Reimagined and restored by award-winning architects WOHA, this exquisite boutique hotel offers an unforgettable stay with its garden terrace and rooftop infinity pool.

### The Warehouse Hotel
Giving industrial design a new local spin, this boutique hotel is housed in a restored heritage building on the bank of the Singapore River.

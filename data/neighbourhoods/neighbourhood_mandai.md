## Overview
Ignite your love for nature at Mandai, home to wildlife parks such as the Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, and River Wonders, alongside a wealth of other nature-themed attractions and lush greenery.

## Atmosphere & Themed Experiences

### Imagine Wilder at the Mandai Wildlife Reserve
Step into the Mandai Wildlife Reserve, a world-leading nature and wildlife destination and your window into the wild. From iconic parks to immersive attractions, dining and stays – experience nature in ways you never imagined.

### Walk on the Wildside
Home to Rainforest Wild ASIA, Singapore Zoo, River Wonders, Bird Paradise and Night Safari, Mandai Wildlife Reserve is a must-visit destination for nature lovers.

### Greenery Galore
Whether you're looking to hike or embark a walking tour, you'll find endless green spaces and nature trails to explore.

### Diverse Sights and Sounds
From hot springs and reservoir parks to wetlands teeming with life, there are sights aplenty to discover off the beaten track.

## What to Do

### Rainforest Wild Adventure
Glide through the canopy or swing high into the sky with Adventure+ activities, taking in heart-racing views while spotting animals beneath. Don't miss the exclusive Okapi and Diana Monkey, now in Singapore for the first time, and many more!

### Exploria
Explore one of Southeast Asia’s largest indoor, nature-themed multimedia attractions where guests​ embark on immersive digital expeditions to rarely seen realms of the world.

### Curiosity Cove
Discover Singapore’s largest indoor nature-inspired playscape for kids 12 and under. Through interactive and educational play, children explore how animals live, adapt and thrive in the wild across four immersive zones filled with wonder and discovery.

### Night Safari
Discover the world's first nocturnal wildlife park, a one-of- a-kind experience that unveils the mysteries of the animal kingdom at night. Embark on a journey by tram or on foot and get up-close to over 900 nocturnal animals.

### Singapore Zoo
Visit Singapore's only rainforest zoo, renowned for its naturalist, cageless habitats. From the furry and adorable to the ferocious and majestic, the animals of the Singapore Zoo are a sight to behold.

### Mandai Gallery
Step into Mandai Gallery, an exhibition that celebrates the history and transformation of the Mandai Wildlife Reserve. Learn about how the reserve has contributed to nation-building, its commitment to sustainability and its efforts in wildlife conservation both locally and regionally.

### Bird Paradise
Take flight to Asia's largest bird park, home to more than 3,500 birds from over 400 species. Explore 10 immersive walk-through aviaries, inspired by habitats from around the world.

### River Wonders
Immerse yourself in Asia's first and only river-themed wildlife park, home to one of the world's largest collections of freshwater vertebrates. Explore iconic rivers and encounter resident giants like the pandas, Mekong catfish and more.

### Kranji War Memorial
Learn more about the trials, triumphs and tribulations of our nation during the Second World War.

### Sembawang Hot Springs
Feet feeling the ache from all that exploring? Kick back and soak in relaxation at the Sembawang Hot Springs.

### Sungei Buloh Coastal Trail 
Encompassing Kranji Marshes, Kranji Reservoir and Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserves, this scenic trail is perfect for avid hikers and photographers.

### Hay Dairies
Visit Hay Dairies, the only goat farm in Singapore producing fresh goat milk locally.

### Kranji Marshes
One of Singapore's largest freshwater marshes, Kranji Marshes is home to an array of plants and animals.

### Mandai Boardwalk
Soak in the scenic views of Upper Seletar Reservoir at Mandai Boardwalk. This easy 3.3km trail invites all ages and abilities to unwind in nature among our native wildlife.

## Where to Shop

### Bollywood Veggies
Taste the freshness of produce proudly grown in Singapore at Bollywood Veggies, a farm in the tranquil countryside.

### The Local Farm
Shop for the freshest local produce or yummy food products like gelato and chowder.

### Thow Kang Pottery
Bring home traditional ceramics from Singapore's oldest dragon kiln, or try your hand at pottery-making.

## Where to Eat

### Kidzworld Café
Grab a bite with your kids at this forest-inspired café that serves a range of delightful bites, from Asian to Western food. Think noodles, rice bowls, fish and chips, pastas.

### Cavern Restaurant
Marvel at the towering rock formations while savouring a feast for the senses at Singapore's first and only restaurant in a cave setting.

### Breakfast in the Wild, Singapore Zoo
Enjoy a planet-friendly breakfast buffet spread in the charming rainforest zoo and meet wildlife ambassadors such as the Orangutans, African Penguins, Sealion, Bengal Eagle Owl and more in this iconic dining experience.

### Crimson Restaurant, Bird Paradise
Elevate your dining experience with a spectacular view of Scarlet Macaws flying through the Hong Leong Foundation Crimson Wetlands against the backdrop of Bird Paradise's tallest waterfall.

### Penguin Cove Restaurant, Bird Paradise
Located in the Ocean Network Express Penguin Cove, savour a delicious selection of western cuisine while watching the penguins take a dive in the pool.

### Dining Alley, Mandai Wildlife EAST
Where dining meets adventure. From iconic local delights at Song Fa Bak Kut Teh to wood-fired pizzas at Brooklyn's famed Roberta's, every bite is a journey.

## Where to Stay

### Glamping in the Wild
Choose between two signature all-inclusive glamping stays – Reservoir Retreat or Crocodile Lodge, complete with park admissions, activities, meals, and guided tours at the Mandai Wildlife Reserve.

### Mandai Rainforest Resort by Banyan Tree
Nestled within the Mandai Wildlife Reserve, the nature- inspired resort offers over 300 rooms, including treehouses that offer stunning views of Upper Seletar Reservoir and feature private patios.

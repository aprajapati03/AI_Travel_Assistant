# Singapore Featured Neighbourhoods Directory

**Source Title**: Featured Neighbourhoods Guide - Visit Singapore  
**URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/  

Singapore is a city of distinct, vibrant districts and enclaves, each offering unique heritage, architectural character, dining gems, shopping streets, and religious monuments. This directory catalogs all 11 official featured neighbourhoods from Visit Singapore.

---

## Complete Catalog of Featured Neighbourhoods

### 1. Civic District
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_civic_district.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/
- **Nearest MRT**: City Hall (NS25/EW13), Esplanade (CC3), Marina Bay (NS27/CE2/TE20)
- **Character**: Historical colonial heart, government monuments, world-class art museums, grand architecture.
- **Highlights**: National Gallery Singapore, National Museum of Singapore, Peranakan Museum, Asian Civilisations Museum, Victoria Theatre & Concert Hall, Raffles Hotel, Lau Pa Sat, and historic Padang.

### 2. Chinatown
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_chinatown.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/
- **Nearest MRT**: Chinatown (NE4/DT19), Maxwell (TE18), Telok Ayer (DT18)
- **Character**: Chinese cultural heritage, historic temples alongside hip cocktail bars and restored shophouses.
- **Highlights**: Buddha Tooth Relic Temple & Museum, Sri Mariamman Temple (Singapore's oldest Hindu temple), Masjid Jamae (Chulia Mosque), Chinatown Complex Food Centre, Maxwell Food Centre, Smith Street night market, and Ann Siang Hill nightlife.

### 3. Kampong Gelam
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_kampong_gelam.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/kampong-gelam/
- **Nearest MRT**: Bugis (EW12/DT14), Jalan Besar (DT22)
- **Character**: Malay-Arab historic quarter, Islamic heritage, bohemian street art, indie boutiques.
- **Highlights**: Sultan Mosque (Masjid Sultan), Haji Lane street art and indie fashion, Arab Street textile and carpet merchants, Bussorah Street cafes, Hjh Maimunah Nasi Padang, and traditional perfumeries like Jamal Kazura Aromatics.

### 4. Katong-Joo Chiat
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_katong_joo_chiat.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/katong-joo-chiat/
- **Nearest MRT**: Marine Parade (TE26), Dakota (CC9), Eunos (EW7)
- **Character**: Peranakan (Straits Chinese) and Eurasian enclave, pastel heritage shophouses, rich foodie haven.
- **Highlights**: Koon Seng Road pastel shophouses, Kim Choo Kueh Chang (Peranakan dumplings and beaded slippers), Chin Mee Chin Confectionery, 328 Katong Laksa, The Intan private heritage museum, and artisanal cafes.

### 5. Little India
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_little_india.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/little-india/
- **Nearest MRT**: Little India (NE7/DT12), Farrer Park (NE8), Rochor (DT13)
- **Character**: Vibrant South Asian heritage, fragrant spice shops, dazzling flower garlands, bustling bazaars.
- **Highlights**: Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, Sri Srinivasa Perumal Temple, Sakya Muni Buddha Gaya Temple (Temple of 1,000 Lights), Tekka Centre hawker food and fresh produce, 24-hour Mustafa Centre, and colorful Tan Teng Niah residence.

### 6. Mandai
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_mandai.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/mandai/
- **Nearest MRT**: Khatib (NS14) with Mandai Shuttle Bus, Springleaf (TE4)
- **Character**: Premier wildlife sanctuary, nature reserves, lush tropical flora and fauna.
- **Highlights**: Mandai Wildlife Reserve featuring Bird Paradise, Singapore Zoo, River Wonders (giant pandas), Night Safari (world's first nocturnal wildlife park), and tranquil Upper Seletar Reservoir.

### 7. Marina Bay
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_marina_bay.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/marina-bay/
- **Nearest MRT**: Bayfront (CE1/DT16), Marina Bay (NS27/CE2/TE20), Promenade (CC4/DT15)
- **Character**: Futuristic skyline, cutting-edge architecture, luxury integrated resort, waterfront promenade.
- **Highlights**: Marina Bay Sands (SkyPark, infinity pool, luxury shoppes), Gardens by the Bay (Supertree Grove, Flower Dome, Cloud Forest), Singapore Flyer, ArtScience Museum, Esplanade - Theatres on the Bay, and Spectra Light & Water Show.

### 8. Orchard Road
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_orchard_road.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/orchard-road/
- **Nearest MRT**: Orchard (NS22/TE14), Somerset (NS23), Dhoby Ghaut (NS24/NE6/CC1)
- **Character**: Asia's premier 2.2 km shopping boulevard, designer flagships, lifestyle malls, urban dining.
- **Highlights**: ION Orchard, Takashimaya / Ngee Ann City, Paragon, Design Orchard (showcasing local Singapore designers), Emerald Hill historic Peranakan shophouse bars, and Tanglin lifestyle precinct.

### 9. Sentosa Island
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_sentosa.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/sentosa-island/
- **Nearest MRT / Transit**: HarbourFront (NE1/CC29) -> Sentosa Express Monorail or Singapore Cable Car
- **Character**: Tropical resort island, adrenaline thrills, sandy beaches, family entertainment.
- **Highlights**: Resorts World Sentosa, Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, Adventure Cove Waterpark, Skyline Luge, Siloso Beach, Tanjong Beach Club, Mega Adventure Park, and Fort Siloso.

### 10. Singapore River
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_singapore_river.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/singapore-river/
- **Nearest MRT**: Clarke Quay (NE5), Fort Canning (DT20), Raffles Place (NS26/EW14)
- **Character**: Historic commercial waterway turned lively dining, bar, and nightlife strip.
- **Highlights**: Clarke Quay nightlife and live music venues, Boat Quay historic shophouse riverside dining, Robertson Quay relaxed cafes and wine bars, and traditional bumboat river cruises.

### 11. Dempsey Hill
- **File**: `data/neighbourhoods/neighbourhood_dempsey.md`
- **Specific URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/dempsey/
- **Nearest MRT**: Napier (TE12), Farrer Road (CC20)
- **Character**: Tranquil lifestyle enclave set among lush colonial British army barracks and rainforest canopy.
- **Highlights**: COMO Dempsey (Candlenut Michelin Peranakan restaurant, Dover Street Market fashion), Dempsey Cookhouse & Bar by Jean-Georges Vongerichten, antiquities and art galleries, and upscale weekend brunch spots.

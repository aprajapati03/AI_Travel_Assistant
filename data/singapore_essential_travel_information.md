# Essential Travel Information for Singapore

**Source Title**: Essential Travel Information - Visit Singapore  
**URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-tips/essential-travel-information/  

---

## Overview

Plan your journey with essential travel tips covering local weather, visa rules, emergency contacts, local regulations, digital payments, and public transportation in Singapore.

---

## 1. General Travel Essentials

### Weather & Timezone
Enjoy summer all year-round with Singapore's tropical climate (25–33°C / 77–91°F). Dress in light, airy clothing, and carry an umbrella or raincoat in case of sudden tropical showers.  
Singapore Standard Time (SST) operates in the **GMT +8** time zone.

### Visa & Entry Requirements
While the majority of international visitors do not require an entry visa, all travelers must submit the electronic **SG Arrival Card (SGAC)** with health declaration within three days prior to arrival. Always check the latest entry requirements and advisories on the official [Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) Portal](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore/visa_requirements).

### Smoking Regulations
Smoking is strictly prohibited in all indoor buildings, retail food establishments (hawker centres, restaurants, and cafes), transport nodes, public transport vehicles, and outdoor sports facilities. Smoking is only permitted in clearly demarcated designated smoking areas (marked with yellow boxes or signs). Refer to the National Environment Agency (NEA) for prohibited areas and enforcement penalties.

### E-Vaporisers & Vaping Ban
The purchase, possession, import, or use of e-vaporisers (e-cigarettes, vapes, and e-liquids) is **strictly illegal** in Singapore. Offenders face heavy composition fines or criminal prosecution. Visitors caught purchasing, possessing, or using e-vaporisers laced with illicit substances such as etomidate ("K-pods") may be refused entry or permanently banned from re-entering Singapore. Do not bring any vaping devices or accessories into the country. Refer to [Gov.sg Stop Vaping](https://www.gov.sg/stopvaping/) for official advisories.

### Safe Drinking Water
Tap water in Singapore easily surpasses World Health Organization (WHO) potable water standards. Tap water is safe to drink directly from the tap without boiling or filtration. Bottled water is also widely available at convenience stores and supermarkets.

### Power Plug & Electricity
- **Voltage**: Standard 220–240 Volts AC at 50 Hz.
- **Plug Type**: Type G three-pronged rectangular blade plug (the standard British 3-pin plug). Universal adapters are recommended if your devices use Type A, C, or other configurations.

### Language & Singlish
English is the main working language and is spoken fluently by almost all Singaporeans. Many residents are bilingual and also speak Mandarin Chinese, Malay, or Tamil. You will also encounter **Singlish**, Singapore's unique colloquial English creole incorporating vocabulary and grammar from Malay, Hokkien, Cantonese, and Tamil (e.g., *"lah"*, *"chope"*, *"shiok"*).

### Useful & Emergency Phone Numbers
- **Police (Emergency)**: `999`
- **Ambulance & Fire Brigade**: `995`
- **Non-Emergency Ambulance**: `1777`
- **Police Hotline (Non-Emergency)**: `1800 255 0000`
- **Changi Airport Flight Information**: `1800 542 4422` / `+65 6542 4422`
- **Tourist Helpline**: `1800 736 2000` (toll-free in Singapore) / `+65 6736 2000` (overseas)

### Prayer Facilities
Singapore is a Muslim-friendly travel destination. Dedicated prayer facilities (surau / musollah) are available across Changi Airport, major shopping malls, theme parks, and tourist attractions. Over 70 mosques are located throughout the city; prayer times and locations can be verified through the [Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura (MUIS)](https://www.muis.gov.sg/community/mosque/).

### Free Public Wi-Fi (Wireless@SGx)
Foreign visitors can connect to free high-speed public Wi-Fi across thousands of **Wireless@SGx** hotspots throughout Singapore (including MRT stations, shopping malls, libraries, and public buildings). Download the Wireless@SGx app and register using an international mobile number to receive an SMS one-time PIN (OTP).

### Singapore Visitor Centres
For in-person assistance, itinerary planning, attraction tickets, or maps, drop by official Singapore Visitor Centres located at:
- **orchardgateway** (Orchard Road)
- **Funan** (Civic District)
- **Kampong Gelam** (55 Bussorah Street)
- **Changi Airport** terminals

### Tray Return Law at Public Dining Places
It is a legally enforced offence to leave dirty trays, used crockery, and litter on tables at hawker centres, food courts, and coffeeshops. Diners are required to clear their own tables and return trays and bowls to designated Tray Return Stations after meals. First-time offenders receive a written warning, while repeat offenders face statutory fines.

## 2. Payment & Money Matters

### Payment Methods & Contactless Transactions
Singapore is largely a cashless society. Contactless payments are ubiquitous across shopping malls, restaurants, supermarkets, and taxis:
- **Contactless Bank Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and JCB credit and debit cards.
- **Mobile Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay.
- **Local QR & Debit**: NETS, GrabPay, and PayNow QR.
- **Cash**: While digital payment is preferred, small amounts of cash in Singapore Dollars (SGD) are useful for small hawker stalls.

### Tipping Norms
Tipping is **not customary or expected** in Singapore. Most sit-down restaurants and hotels automatically add a **10% service charge** and the prevailing Goods & Services Tax (GST) to the final bill. However, tipping for exemplary service is welcomed and appreciated.

### Tourist Tax Refund (eTRS - 9% GST Refund)
Tourists can claim a refund on the **9% Goods and Services Tax (GST)** paid on eligible purchases:
- **Eligibility Requirement**: Spend a minimum of **SGD 100** (inclusive of GST) at participating retailers displaying the "Tax Free" shopping logo. You may accumulate up to three same-day receipts from the same retailer to meet the SGD 100 minimum.
- **Electronic Tourist Refund Scheme (eTRS)**: Present your passport to the merchant when making purchases to have your transaction electronically tagged to your passport.
- **Claiming the Refund**: Before checking in your luggage at Changi Airport or Seletar Airport, use the automated **eTRS self-help kiosks** to scan your passport and claim your refund via credit card or cash.

## 3. Getting Around: Public Transport & Taxis

### Ways to Pay for Public Transit
Singapore's public transit network (MRT trains and public buses) uses an integrated fare system:
1. **Contactless Bank Cards & Mobile Wallets**: Simply tap your contactless Mastercard, Visa credit/debit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay at fare gates and bus card readers. (Foreign card administrative transaction fees may apply).
2. **Singapore Tourist Pass (STP)**: A special EZ-Link stored-value card offering tourists unlimited travel on public buses and MRT trains for 1, 2, or 3 consecutive days.
3. **Stored-Value Cards (EZ-Link / NETS FlashPay)**: Available at transit customer service counters and 7-Eleven outlets.

### Accessibility Options
All MRT train stations and public buses in Singapore are fully wheelchair accessible and barrier-free, equipped with ramps, tactile floor guidance, wide fare gates, and station lifts. Open baby strollers and prams are permitted on all public buses and trains.

### MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) Network
The MRT is the quickest and most efficient way to travel across Singapore:
- Operates daily from approximately **05:30 AM to 12:00 midnight**.
- Train frequency ranges from 2 to 3 minutes during peak commute hours and 5 to 7 minutes off-peak.
- Train routes, real-time arrival timings, and network maps are accessible via the official **MyTransport.SG** app (available on iOS and Android) or the Land Transport Authority (LTA) website.

### Public Bus Network
Public bus services (operated by SBS Transit, SMRT Buses, Tower Transit, and Go-Ahead Singapore) connect virtually every neighborhood across the island:
- Buses operate from **05:30 AM to approximately 12:30 midnight**.
- Remember to tap your card or mobile wallet on the card reader upon boarding **and tap again before exiting** through the rear doors to ensure correct distance-based fare deduction.

### Metered Taxis & Surcharges
Taxis in Singapore are metered, regulated, air-conditioned, and safe:
- Available at taxi stands located outside shopping malls, hotels, and MRT stations, or flagged along street kerbs (outside restricted bus lanes).
- **Fare Surcharges**: Extra surcharges apply depending on timing and location:
  - *Peak Period Surcharge*: +25% of metered fare during morning and evening rush hours.
  - *Midnight Surcharge*: +50% of metered fare between 12:00 midnight and 05:59 AM.
  - *Location Surcharges*: Apply at Changi Airport, Seletar Airport, Marina Bay Sands, Mandai Wildlife Reserve, and Sentosa Island.
  - Always request a printed or electronic receipt from the driver at the end of the journey.

### Ride-Hailing Applications
If you prefer booking rides via smartphone apps with upfront fixed fares:
- **Grab**: Most widely used ride-hailing and food delivery platform in Southeast Asia.
- **Gojek**: Popular competitive on-demand car ride booking app.
- **CDG Zig (ComfortDelGro)**: Official app of Singapore's largest taxi fleet, booking both metered taxis and private-hire cars.
- **TADA**: Zero-commission ride-hailing platform popular with local drivers.
- **Ryde**: Local carpooling and private ride-hailing app.

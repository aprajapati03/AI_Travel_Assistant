# Wellness Escapades in Singapore

**Source Title**: Wellness Escapades in Singapore - Visit Singapore
**URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-tips/travelling-to-singapore/itineraries/relaxing-things-to-do-in-singapore/
**Duration**: Wellness & Relaxation

---

## Overview
Find new ways to relax, rejuvenate and revitalise your body and spirit during your tropical vacation to the Lion City.


## Day 1
*Gardens and hip cafes.*

#### Singapore Botanic Gardens
Recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, some highlights include the National Orchid Garden, a gorgeous swan lake and tropical trees to take in the beauty of nature.

#### Prairie By Craftsmen
Dig into delectable brunches, beverages and desserts in this gorgeous glasshouse cafe surrounded by nature and natural sunlight.

#### Duxton Hill
If you're looking to unwind, this neighbourhood is home to a range of hidden gems. Find traditional traditional shophouse architecture, quaint boutiques and buzzing cafes and bars.

#### Trapeze Rec Club
A one stop holistic wellness club in the city. Discover four floors of wellness classes from yoga and martial arts to recovery sessions.


## Day 2
*Enjoy a slower pace of Singapore.*

#### Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park
This 62-hectare park is home to a variety of fauna and flora, pond gardens and river plains and is a must visit for those looking for respite in the city.

#### Como Shambhala
A multi-storey lifestyle destination featuring a range of wellness and fitness offerings, from mindfulness and sound healing to pilates classes, Como Shambhala will cater to your wellness journey.

#### Dempsey Hill
A lesser-known locale home to luxury shops and stellar dining options, this heritage site is now an idyllic lifestyle destination and offers a slower pace of life.


## Day 3
*Escape the city.*

#### Pulau Ubin
Nature and history intertwine on Pulau Ubin, located off Singapore’s north-eastern coast. This tranquil island sanctuary is a popular haunt of nature lovers and day trippers unwinding from the bustle of the city.

#### Mangrove Kayaking
Beginner friendly, this guided tour allows you to explore the Pulau Ubin's tranquil environment and abundance of floral and fauna whilst paddling on kayaks.

#### Lazarus Island
Alternatively, head to Lazarus Island located in the South, for pristine beaches, quiet picnics and a tranquil island getaway.

#### Dusit Thani Laguna
Relax in your luxurious suite at Dusit Thani Laguna, before teeing on the idyllic greens of the golf course and pampering yourself to an array of facials and massages at Devarana Wellness.


## Day 4
*Swing by some of the city's icons.*

#### Buddha Tooth Relic Temple
Located in the historic district of Chinatown, this majestic place of worship is a repository of Buddhist wisdom and relics, and is home to an artefact of religious reverence.

#### ArtScience Museum 
Catch world-class touring exhibitions at this premier exhibition space, where the wondrous worlds of art and science collide.

#### Gardens By the Bay
Revel in the spectacular views from the Supertree Grove amid lush gardens. Escape from the tropical heat at the cooled conservatories.

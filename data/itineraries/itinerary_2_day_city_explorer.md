### A City Explorer's Guide to Singapore
Embark on a 2-day journey in Singapore, immersing yourself in a blend of cultural experiences, museum visits, attractions, through Singapore’s diverse cityscape.


## Day 1
*Be enchanted by the sights and sounds of the Civic District.*

#### National Museum Singapore
Start your day by learning about the history and culture of Singapore at the city's oldest museum.

#### Singapore Art Museum 
Explore contemporary art at the Singapore Art Museum situated at the historic port area of Tanjong Pagar.

#### Maxwell Food Centre 
Enjoy a local meal at a hawker centre, dig into a succulent plate of Michelin Bib Gourmand Tian Tian Hainanese chicken rice, while sipping on an ice cold sugar cane juice.

#### Gardens by the Bay
Catch a breather at the Cloud Forest, an indoor cooled conservatory that features a lush mountain clad and exotic plant species, or head to the Flower Dome for floral displays in this mega glass greenhouse.

#### Esplanade - Theatres on the Bay
A world-class performing arts centre made up of over 7,000 triangular aluminium sunshades that resembles the durian fruit is a must visit for any art lover.

#### Merlion Park
Commemorate your visit to The Lion City with a photo of the half fish, half lion creature – the majestic Merlion.


## Day 2
*A thrilling blend of arts, culture and adventure.*

#### Chinatown
Explore this vibrant neighborhood, where you can visit the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Museum, Sri Mariamman Temple and Masjid Jamae Mosque.

#### Haji Lane
Take a stroll along the colorful streets of Haji Lane in the Kampong Gelam district, known for its trendy boutiques and street art.

#### Asian Civilisations Museum
Discover myths, traditions and artefacts from our region's storied past with immersive activities, multimedia showcases and more.

#### Singapore River
Take a river cruise along the Singapore River to enjoy the scenic views and learn about the city's history.

#### Night Safari
As dusk falls, embark on an adventure at an open air zoo that houses over 2,500 nocturnal animals.

### Honeymoon in Singapore
Whether you’re newly married or celebrating milestones, Singapore is the perfect destination for couples in the mood for love, with romantic experiences like a spa session and an open-air sky bar setting the scene.


## Day 1
*Beauty and art in the city’s heart.*

#### National Gallery Singapore
This museum makes a unique date for art lovers. Marvel at impressive sculptures and masterpieces at one of the world’s largest public collections of Southeast Asian modern art.

#### Odette
Housed within the National Gallery, Michelin-starred Odette is the perfect place for a special meal out, with its Modern French dishes.

#### Gardens by the Bay
If you are both into nature, visit the magical Supertrees and catch a breather at the indoor Flower Dome and Cloud Forest.

#### Marguerite
Spend quality time with your loved one at Marguerite, a fine dining restaurant set right in the heart of the Flower Dome at Gardens by the Bay.

#### Raffles Hotel
Renowned for its colonial architecture, this hotel offers a beautiful setting for every romantic celebration. Enjoy a relaxing high tea session in the Grand Lobby, and book a history tour to learn more about Singapore’s oldest hotel.

#### CÉ LA VI 
Looking for a romantic evening with stunning city views? End the night by savouring cocktails at CÉ LA VI, perched on the 57th floor of Marina Bay Sands.


## Day 2
*Cultural precincts and party spots.*

#### Chinatown
Lovers of culture looking to immerse themselves in Singapore’s rich culture will find no better place than this vibrant neighbourhood, where you can visit the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Masjid Jame Mosque.

#### Kok Sen 
If you and your date are fans of traditional Chinese fare, check out this traditional coffeeshop, which is a constant on the Singapore Michelin Bib Gourmand list.

#### Level Up
Take your date night to the next level with Level Up, Singapore’s first ever arcade-bar. It is a dream come true for couples who enjoy video games and are up for a challenge.

#### Atlas
Designed to mimic a grand hotel with a Great Gatsby-esque feel, Atlas has one of the largest gin libraries in the world. Be sure to snag a seat here with your significant other.

#### Kulnari Mystery Golf
Hole in one meets mysterious historical intrigue at Kulnari Mystery Golf. This mini-golf course is a treasure trove of puzzles and clues for the adventurous and quirky pair.


## Day 3
*Serenity and romantic seaside retreats.*

#### Sentosa Island
Situated away from Singapore’s bustling cityscape, this island promises white beaches, blue surf and idyllic island life for couples looking to escape the urban buzz.

#### Singapore Oceanarium
Journey into the realms beneath the waves at Singapore Oceanarium at Resorts World Sentosa, and visit the viewing panel which holds over 40,000 marine animals across 120 species.

#### Auriga Spa
Located at Capella Singapore, this spa offers everything from facials for men, to body wraps and massages with couple suites that will leave both of you feeling brand new.

#### Mangrove Kayaking
Looking for a memorable adventure? Glide through serene mangroves and encounter diverse wildlife, while connecting with your significant other at Pulau Ubin.

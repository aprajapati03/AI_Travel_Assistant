### Top Outdoor Adventure Activities in Singapore
Calling all nature lovers, thrill-seekers, and outdoor enthusiasts to dive into Singapore's wilder side with this 4-day guide, packed with land- and water-based adventures.


## Day 1
*Indulge in idyllic island life.*

#### Pulau Ubin
Located on the eastern end of Singapore, hitch a bumboat ride to find rustic shrines, swamplands and scenic summits. 
 This sanctuary is the perfect place for explorers looking to unwind and deepen their love for nature.

#### Mangrove Kayaking
Beginner friendly, this guided tour allows you to explore the Pulau Ubin's tranquil environment and abundance of floral and fauna whilst paddling on kayaks.

#### Chek Jawa
Tucked away in the east of Pulau Ubin, this rich intertidal ecosystem is home to diverse wildlife. Head to the Jewawi Tower for a chance to observe birds and a panoramic view of the island.

#### Lazarus Island
As an alternative to Pulau Ubin Island, bask in the sun, sand, and sea at Lazarus Island. This hidden gem boasts relaxing beaches and abundant marine life, just a ferry ride away from the mainland.


## Day 2
*Out and about for nature lovers.*

#### Singapore Botanic Gardens
Recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, find highlights such as National Orchid Garden, a gorgeous swan lake and heaps of tropical trees to please every plant enthusiast out there.

#### Bukit Timah Nature Reserve
While the sun’s still out, check out this reserve to find Singapore’s highest hill, Bukit Timah Hill. Challenge yourself to a trek up its 163-metre-high slopes, on trails that wind through pockets of forested area.

#### Southern Ridges
Keep your camera on hand when you hike the photogenic ten-kilometre-long network of trails. Head to Henderson Waves, the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore.

#### MacRitchie Nature Trail and Reservoir Park
The highlight of this park is its Treetop Walks, a 250-metre aerial free standing suspension bridge spanning the two highest points within MacRitchie.


## Day 3
*Calling all thrill seekers to Sentosa.*

#### AltitudeX
Experience the world’s largest wind tunnel for indoor skydiving. State-of-the-art technology will lend you wings in an experience that simulates free falls from heights of 12,000 to 3,000 feet.

#### Mega Adventure Park
Brave new heights with this adrenaline-pumping zip wire ride, where you will get to experience a whole new perspective of Sentosa's lush jungles and white beaches from 450 metres in the air.

#### Hydrodash
Located along the waters of Palawan Beach, Singapore’s first floating aquapark is an unforgettable experience for the young and the young at heart.

#### Skyline Luge
Embark on a thrilling journey as you zoom down thrilling courses in a 3-wheeled luge. 
 The skyride also offers a magnificent bird’s eye view of the city.

#### Adventure Cove
Have a splashing good time at Adventure Cove, experience thrilling water slides, snorkel with fish, and relax on the lazy river.


## Day 4
*Fitness in the heart of the city.*

#### Gardens By the Bay
Gardens by the Bay is a stunning park consisting of three waterfront gardens. Discover rare flowers, Supertrees, and intriguing sculptures from all over the world.

#### Trifecta
Surf, snow and skate in one destination! This integrated facility in Orchard Road will offer a wave of excitement for both beginners and seasoned professionals.

#### Climb Central Funan
Ascend to new heights with rock climbing located in the centre of this trendy mall. Pick between high intensity short climbs or scale the many high walls.

#### Slingshot
This thrilling ride catapults you nearly 70 metres up in the air, at speeds of over 160 kilometres per hour. Enjoy the breezy nightscape of the Singapore River while screaming at the top of your lungs!

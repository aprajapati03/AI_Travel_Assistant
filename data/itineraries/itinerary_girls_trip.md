# Your Dream Girls Trip in Singapore

**Source Title**: Your Dream Girls Trip in Singapore - Visit Singapore
**URL**: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-tips/travelling-to-singapore/itineraries/bachelorette-party-trip-in-singapore/
**Duration**: 4 Days

---

## Overview
With its reputation as one of the world's safest cities, Singapore lets you explore with confidence, day or night. Whether you're planning an unforgettable bachelorette bash or a fun-filled girls' getaway, this city is the perfect destination to celebrate in style.


## Day 1 - City Sights & First Bites
*Explore Singapore in Style*

#### Singapore Botanic Gardens
Stroll through Singapore’s first UNESCO World Heritage site, filled with vibrant blooms and scenic spots like the National Orchid Garden and Swan Lake.

#### Jumbo Seafood Dempsey
Enjoy a flavourful seafood lunch in the tranquil, lush Dempsey Hill enclave. Don't forget to dig into Singapore's iconic chilli crab while you're there.

#### Vespa Sidecar Tour
Cruise through the city’s charming streets on a stylish Vespa for a fun and photo-worthy ride.

#### CÉ LA VI
Savour dinner with sweeping skyline views at one of Singapore’s most iconic rooftop restaurants.

#### Marquee
Cap off the night dancing under the lights at Marquee, Singapore’s ultimate nightlife destination.


## Day 2 - Seas the Day
*A Luxe Boat Day on the Water*

#### Luxury Charter Singapore
Sail away in style with your crew on a private yacht—sun, sea, and fun bonding vibes guaranteed.

#### Tanjong Beach Club
Kick back with coastal vibes, fresh bites, and beachside cocktails—the recipe for a chic, sun-kissed midday hangout.

#### Humpback
Share small plates and fresh oysters at this cosy, stylish seafood spot, perfect for catching up over great food and even better conversation.

#### Jigger & Pony
Toast to the night with expertly crafted cocktails at a swanky, award-winning bar that sets the scene for girl time.


## Day 3 – A Day of Delicate Pleasures
*Perfume-Making + Afternoon Tea Delight*

#### Oo La Lab
Design bespoke scents with your besties in a fun, hands-on workshop that’s equal parts personal and playful.

#### The Grand Lobby at Raffles Singapore
Indulge in a special afternoon tea, where delicate treats and the elegant setting set the stage for a luxe girls' day.

#### Gardens By The Bay
Wander through futuristic floral displays and towering Supertrees for a magical, photo-ready adventure with the girls.

#### Mama Diam
Kick off your girls’ night at Mama Diam, a retro-inspired speakeasy serving up bold local bites and creative cocktails with a nostalgic twist.

#### Atlas
Step into the art deco splendour of Atlas and raise a toast with your girls to cap off a fabulous night out.


## Day 4 – Zen & Zest
*Finding Your Balance Between Self-Care and Street Eats*

#### Off Duty Pilates
Start the day strong with a sleek, energising workout that sets the tone for a wellness-focused girls’ escape.

#### Hideaway
From massages to contrast therapy, unwind together with a pampering spa session in a serene setting, because self-care is better with company.

#### Maxwell Food Center
Dive into Singapore's rich food culture, where Michelin Bib gems and beloved traditional eats make it ideal for sharing and soaking up the city's vibrant hawker scene.

#### Dusk at Mount Faber Peak
With golden hour views and mouthwatering bites, Dusk is the ultimate backdrop for mountaintop charm and carefree laughs.

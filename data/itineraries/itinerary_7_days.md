# Enjoy Singapore in 7 Days

**Duration**: 7 Days

## Overview
With a week to spare, discover how east meets west, nature and urban beauty intertwine, and the past meets present, with our guide to Singapore's most iconic spots.

### Enjoy Singapore In 7 Days
With a week to spare, discover how east meets west, nature and urban beauty intertwine, and the past meets present, with our guide to Singapore's most iconic spots.


## Day 1: Urban Nature, Modern Marvels


### Morning

#### Marina Bay 
An iconic district in Singapore, that's guaranteed to bombard your senses, with a mix of nature, arts and shopping, there's something for everyone.

#### Gardens by the Bay
Spend the day marveling at the flora on display at the Flower Dome, and exploring the mist-filled landscape of the Cloud Forest.

#### Marina Bay Sands & The Shoppes
Explore floors of luxury retailers, with the largest collection of coveted designer boutiques in Asia.


### Afternoon

#### Kok Kee Wonton Noodles 
Break for lunch - dig into fresh and springy noodles and enjoy a beloved hawker staple at this popular establishment.

#### ArtScience Museum
Calling all art enthusiasts, this beloved lotus-inspired establishment blurs the lines between art, science, culture and technology, with exciting exhibitions on rotation.

#### Merlion Park
Take a stroll over to snap photos of our city’s most famous icon, the Merlion - with a body of a fish and the head of a lion.


### Evening

#### Lau Pa Sat
With its charming colonial architecture and bustling atmosphere, relish in this restored Victorian food market that offers a quintessential taste of Singapore.

#### LeVeL 33
End the night off with a mug of artisanal beer and award-winning rooftop dining concept at the world’s highest microbrewery with a stunning view of Marina Bay.

#### CÉ LA VI
Perched on the 57th floor of Marina Bay Sands, savour signature cocktails while taking in the stunning city views from the top.


## Day 2: Thrills and Spills at Sentosa


### Morning

#### Sentosa
Sun, sand and surf await you on your second day. Located off our city's southern shore, Sentosa island is known for its white beaches and family-friendly activities.

#### Universal Studios Singapore
Experience the magic of the movies at this theme park, home to a host of exhilarating rides, spread across six movie-themed districts from Hollywood to The Lost World.

#### Singapore Oceanarium
Alternatively, embark on an odyssey beneath the waves and find captivating species from the deep, including Atlantic Sea Nettles, Fried Egg Sea Jellies and more.


### Afternoon

#### Tanjong Beach Club 
Each of Sentosa's beaches possess their own unique charms, from tranquil Tanjong Beach to the hustle and bustle of Siloso Beach.

#### Quentin's Eurasian Restaurant
Alternatively, find delectable dishes like curry debal and buah keluak fried rice at this restaurant serving up authentic Eurasian fare.

#### The Palawan @Sentosa
With a floating aqua park, an indoor electric go-kart arena, mini-golf and more, this beach side wonderland promises fun for the entire family.


### Evening

#### Royal Albatross
Dine and sail on an evening cruise with Royal Albatross, or lunch at the Albatross Island Restaurant in the Southern Islands, reachable by Albatross speedboats from Marina South Pier, Resorts World Sentosa, or Ola Beach Club.

#### Singapore Cable Car
Travel to and from Sentosa in style, with stunning aerial views on board the Singapore Cable Car.

#### VivoCity
End your day at this stunning waterfront view mall that offers a plethora of retail, entertainment, and lifestyle experiences.


## Day 3: Culture and Heritage


### Morning

#### Civic District
Singapore’s rich colonial history and bright future intertwine in the Civic District, where museums and colonial buildings sit alongside skyscrapers.

#### National Museum of Singapore
Explore our city's culture and heritage at this grand old dame of a local museum, where history comes to life.

#### National Gallery Singapore
Housed in buildings that were once our country's City Hall and Supreme Court, National Gallery Singapore boasts over 8,000 works of art.


### Afternoon

#### Bugis Street Market
Explore Bugis, a district with a colourful past now known for its trendy stores. Find great bargains and more at Bugis Street Market.

#### Little India 
Make a beeline for the ethnic enclave Little India, and tuck into mouth-watering South Indian food such as popular local delicacy fish head curry at Banana Leaf Apolo.

#### Mustafa Centre
Itching for more shopping options? check out Mustafa Centre – a shopping complex that sells just about everything you can think of.


### Evening

#### St Andrew's Cathedral 
Singapore's largest cathedral is a stellar example of colonial-era architecture and heritage.

#### Chijmes
Formerly a Catholic convent, this timeless landmark is now home to various restaurants and cafes like Whitegrass, New Ubin Seafood and The Glasshouse.

#### Raffles Hotel 
Don't forget to swing by this legendary colonial-style hotel which exudes timeless elegance, as well as the Long Bar.


## Day 4: Wildlife Awaits


### Morning

#### Mandai
Make your way to Northern Singapore to ignite your love for nature with the majestic animals, nature-themed attractions and lush greenery at Mandai.

#### Bird Paradise
Home to over 3,500 birds, be immersed in naturalistic walk-through aviaries that reflect different habitats of the world.

#### Singapore Zoo
Experience the wonders of wildlife from the furry and adorable to the ferocious and majestic, the animals of the Singapore Zoo are a sight to behold.


### Evening

#### Mandai Wildlife West
Play, dine or enjoy a quick respite from your wildlife adventure, from the towering waterfall upon arrival to the interactive play areas.

#### Evening in the Wild
Dig into a four-course dinner in Singapore's largest Tipi Tent and embark on a tram ride to discover the mysterious creatures of the night.

#### Night Safari
As night falls, head over to the world’s first-ever park for nocturnal animals and embark on walking trails.


## Day 5: Hip Meets Heritage


### Morning

#### Chinatown
Take in the sights and sounds of Chinatown, and head to the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Masjid Jamae Mosque and Sri Mariamman Temple.

#### Chinatown Complex Food Centre
Taste our various cultures, from sweet to savoury, local to international, at the largest hawker centre in Singapore!

#### Tong Heng Traditional Cantonese Pastries
Some of the toothsome pastries from Tong Heng include candied melon pastries, savoury buns and their signature egg tarts.


### Afternoon

#### Chinatown Street Market
More than just a shopping stretch, this iconic street is home to a wide range of offerings, from lacquered fans to charming souvenirs.

#### Lim Chee Guan
Bring home a traditional taste of Singapore. Bak kwa (barbequed meat slices) is a delectable dried sweet and savoury snack similar to jerky.

#### Mei Hong Yuen Dessert
Beat the heat and enjoy a selection of traditional desserts from this beloved eatery. Be sure to try their snow ice and mango sago.


### Evening

#### Kampong Gelam
Pop by this historic neighbourhood from vibrant street art to the majestic charm of the Sultan Mosque, this is a melting pot of cultures both hip and historical.

#### Sultan Mosque
The golden-domed Sultan Mosque — Kampong Gelam’s most striking landmark —makes for a beautiful photo opportunity at dusk.

#### Haji Lane
A bustling narrow lane adorned with colourful murals, boutique shops, and trendy cafes. Stroll through this street, known for its boutiques, cafes and buzzing nightlife.


## Day 6: A Mix of Everything - Nature, Shopping, Party


### Morning

#### Singapore Botanic Gardens 
Spend the morning amidst nature and tranquility at this UNESCO heritage site, which is filled with beautiful flora and fauna.

#### National Orchid Garden
Don’t miss the National Orchid Garden nestled within the Botanic Gardens, home to the largest orchid display in the world with over 60,000 plants and orchids.

#### The Halia 
Break for brunch - located amidst the one-hectare Ginger Garden, this restaurant specialises in contemporary European cuisine.


### Afternoon

#### Orchard Road
Switch up the pace – head to Singapore’s major shopping district, where you’ll find a mix of high-end brands, local labels, and everything in between.

#### Design Orchard
Experience a curation of over 100 homegrown fashion and lifestyle labels, the largest one-stop destination to shop all things local.

#### Emerald Hill
Located amidst the hustle and bustle of Orchard Road, take a stroll to admire the colourful Peranakan shophouses, and enjoy a pitstop at the bars.


### Evening

#### Singapore River
Take a stroll down the Singapore River and experience delectable food and buzzing sights at the heart of Singapore's vibrant nightlife scene.

#### Slingshot
Feel the adrenaline course through your veins with a thrilling ride that’ll have you careening nearly 70 metres through the air at speeds over 160 kilometres per hour.

#### Offtrack
Art, food and music come together at Offtrack, an effortlessly cool bar that serves up Pan Asian cuisine, cocktails and live DJ sets.


## Day 7: Peranakan Delights and Rich Culture


### Morning

#### Katong-Joo Chiat 
Nestled in the East, with its rainbow-hued heritage shophouses, hip cafes and modern boutiques, this is one of Singapore’s most charming districts.

#### 328 Katong Laksa
Indulge in a piping hot bowl of laksa, a spicy coconut milk-based noodle soup rich in flavour, with ingredients such as prawns, cockles and bean sprouts.

#### Kim Choo Kueh Chang
From stunning traditional wares and unique souvenirs, this beloved store is your one-stop shop for discovering the rich tapestry of Peranakan culture.


### Afternoon

#### Cat Socrates 
Shop for home decor, lifestyle items and more. Many of the goods here are designed by artists from Singapore and across Asia, making it a great way to grab a souvenir.

#### Colourful Shophouses Along Koon Seng Road
These iconic, pastel-hued houses date back to the 1920s, and provide the perfect opportunity for you to feed your social media accounts.

#### Eurasian Heritage Gallery
Learn about the history of the Eurasian community in Singapore across the three galleries of this quaint heritage attraction.


### Evening

#### Jewel Changi Airport
An unparalleled fusion of design, architecture and lush nature, this world-class lifestyle hub is the perfect way to end your trip to Singapore.

#### HSBC Rain Vortex and Shiseido Forest Valley
Take a leisurely stroll through the forest and marvel at the centrepiece attraction – the world’s tallest indoor waterfall.

#### Canopy Park 
Head to the top floor of Jewel Changi Airport to rediscover your inner child by exploring mazes, bouncing nets, and giant slides.
